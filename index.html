Nada & Hidde

April 3rd, 2027 Dahab Island Palace, Cairo

Together with their families, Nada Abdelwahab and Hidde Lycklama request the pleasure of your company to celebrate their wedding in Cairo.

Our celebration will take place at Dahab Island Palace, on an island in the Nile. Built in the Mamluk style, the palace sits among palm groves and farmland on Gezirat el-Dahab, a quiet green island just south of central Cairo.
