Make your way to Cairo.

A few practical details to help you settle in, so you can spend more time enjoying the celebration.

Getting here

Fly into Cairo International Airport (CAI) or Sphinx International Airport (SPX). Compare flights to both when planning your trip, and check the journey to your chosen accommodation before booking.

Getting around

We recommend Uber as the most reliable way to get around Cairo. Download the app before you arrive, and leave a little extra time for journeys through the city.
