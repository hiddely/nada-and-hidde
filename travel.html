Getting here

We recommend flying into Cairo International Airport (CAI). If you are planning a longer trip, many holiday airlines offer low-cost flights to Cairo, as well as other cities in Egypt such as Hurghada (HRG) or Luxor (LXR).

Getting around

Uber is the most reliable way to get around Cairo.
